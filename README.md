# Waxline Support

Waxline is an iPhone puzzle game for iOS 26 or later.

## Get help

Email: [canbasci@gmail.com](mailto:canbasci@gmail.com)

Or open an issue on this page:
https://github.com/CanBASCI/Waxline-Support/issues

## Play without Game Center

Menu → Play vs AI. Skip the intro with Skip.

## Live match

Needs two Game Center accounts on two devices, both online. The player who invites opens first.

## Privacy

Waxline does not collect personal data. See [Privacy](PRIVACY.md).
